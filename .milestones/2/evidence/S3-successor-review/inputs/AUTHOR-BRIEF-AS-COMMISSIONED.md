## How to read the SS-0 experiment — corrected, and binding

**1. Do not encode "type mismatch = semantic, tactic failure = proof-script" as a

**not by itself** proof that a theorem statement is false: non-definitional
equality can require another proof. Conversely a tactic failure **may** be the
symptom of a false statement. Bind a **semantic** failure to the actual changed
definition **plus** a satisfying antecedent with a falsified consequent, or an

than the measured cold invocation. That **refutes a universal cheaper-incremental
assumption**; it does **not** establish a stable comparative cost for any future
run of this chain. Keep **actual timer units** and **measured versus extrapolated**
costs distinct throughout. No timing campaign is granted to polish this.
